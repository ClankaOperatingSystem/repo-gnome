=== document methods/purpose.org
#+TITLE: Purpose and service

The maintainer has many competing responsibilities. Design for
interruption, trustworthy delegation and inexpensive resumption. Ask
about lived experience before interpreting silence; a pause in one
project is not evidence of lost interest.
=== document skills/start-up/SKILL.md
# Start up

Capture newly surfaced loose ends in an authorised home without treating
every idea as a commitment. Do not infer loss of interest from
interruption.
