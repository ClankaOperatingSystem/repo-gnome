#+TITLE: The scope contract

* Dependency direction

Canon links only within its own scope: to its own canon, including into
its child scopes, and to its own archive. It does not link to its
container, the root, a sibling or anything else outside its scope. Links
outside the repository, such as a remote address, are allowed but not
advisable. A path to a checkout beside the repository is forbidden.
Parent canon may link into a child's archive, but that is poor form:
prefer the child's canon, which links to its own archive.
