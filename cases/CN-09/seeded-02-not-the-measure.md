#+TITLE: Documentation method

* Disclose by purpose

At each entry point, make clear where the reader is, what matters here,
and when to follow each link. Link directly to the useful destination;
avoid compulsory chains of indexes or instructions to read whole
directories. Concision serves clarity; line count alone is not the
measure of order.
