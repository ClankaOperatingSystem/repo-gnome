# Start up

## Begin deliberately

Choose among doing established work, responding to new work, and
defining work. When the maintainer has not selected work, bring forward a
small set of suitable next actions and explain each one's fit.

## Learn from use

Use the method's evaluation criteria to notice missed obligations, mental
burden, unnecessary ceremony and handoff failures. Record material
evidence at a natural pause or wrap-up. Structural checks and desk
reviews do not establish benefit in use. Refine this skill from observed
use.
