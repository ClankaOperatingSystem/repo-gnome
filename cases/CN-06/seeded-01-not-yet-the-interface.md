#+TITLE: Commands

The =startup-prompt=, =startup-agenda= and =startup-report= commands are a
temporary spike. They print static opening prompts before reading saved
Org files, then exercise provisional agenda file sets and views. They are
not yet the repository's start-up interface or settled agenda
configuration. The spike currently proposes recursive =canon= and
=projects= sets; change those hypotheses during the experiment rather
than treating them as the agenda contract.
