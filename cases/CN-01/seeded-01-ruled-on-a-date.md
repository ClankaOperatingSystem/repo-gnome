#+TITLE: Tickets

Issue trackers and pull requests are information from outside: a channel
between the team and the people who use the software. They are not the
unit of work; next actions are chosen in the project files. A ticket that
bears on a project is referenced from the project file it bears on. Each
tracker is swept daily by a scheduled item in the responsibility that owns
the repository. Ms Okafor ruled this on 2025-03-02.
